What it does

Quiet by design

Every display

One trail across every monitor you have plugged in — including one you connect while it is already running.

Never in the way

The trail is drawn on a layer that cannot be clicked. Your clicks, drags and hovers behave exactly as before.

Above everything

Stays visible over full-screen apps and across every Space, so presentations and screen shares keep the trail.

Lives in the menu bar

No Dock icon, no window to manage. Switch it on once and forget it is there.

Nothing to grant

No accessibility permission, no account, no sign-in. Install it and it simply works.

Ready at login

Optionally starts with your Mac, so the trail is there before you are.
