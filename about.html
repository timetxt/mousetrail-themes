About

A small utility, built and kept by one developer.

MouseTrail draws a fading, colorful trail behind your cursor — on every display, above every app, right from the menu bar. It's built and maintained independently, asks for no account or sign-in, and the Classic Rainbow trail stays free forever.

MouseTrail Pro adds more trail colors and finer length & width controls, if you want them.
